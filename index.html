About Us

A Legacy of Trust in Financial Services

Artline Finance Pvt Ltd (formerly known as Artline Vinimay Pvt Ltd) is a Non-Banking Finance Company (NBFC) incorporated on 9 August 1996 and registered with the Reserve Bank of India (Registration Certificate is B-14.03540). The company is dedicated to empowering individuals and businesses with accessible, transparent, and innovative financial solutions. We provide a wide range of financial lending products, with a specific focus on mortgage backed lending to MSMEs on a short to medium term basis. Artline Finance continuously endeavours to simplify the financial appraisal process and also structure products based on close monitoring of end usage and exit strategy.

Our Mission

Artline Finance is driven by a mission to make financial services transparent, ethical, and accessible. Through its client-centric approach, the company strives to bridge financial resource gaps that are required to build robust and progressive businesses.

Our Vision

The company's vision is to become a leading financial solutions provider recognized for trust, innovation, and integrity with a dedicated commitment towards customer service.

Financing Solutions

Products We Offer

Our lending services empower businesses and individuals to unlock their financial potential with products designed for diverse needs.

🏠

Loan Against Property

Our structured mortgage backed lending product offers a quick turnaround and monthly interest outflow based on cash flow assessment. Artline Finance is proud to offer transparent and seamless customer service experience by offering competitive processing fees and nil foreclosure charges.

  • Easy application and speedy decision
  • Lending up to 75% of the property's market value (at the sole discretion of Artline Finance)
  • Flexible repayment period with doorstep services
  • Flexibility in usage — home improvement, debt consolidation, business expansion, etc.
  • Customised repayment terms aligned with your financial standing
  • Quick processing times for prompt fund access
  • Dedicated customer service throughout the loan process
💳

Unsecured Business Loans

Artline Finance provides unsecured business loans for short to medium tenors based on the end usage of the business.

  • Quick Initial Assessment — technology based appraisal basis the nature of the requirement, repayment capability, and credit history
  • Solutions for Diverse Needs — tailormade product at competitive interest rates and nil preclosure charges
🔗

Vendor & Supplier Finance

Empowering business to support their cash flows for more efficient Supply Chain. Loans against Invoice/Purchase Order/Work Order. Artline Finance offers this product selectively and for a short tenor basis only.

  • Loan against invoice, purchase order, or work order
  • Improved cash flow for suppliers
  • Reduced cost of capital compared to traditional lending
  • Strengthens supplier relationships and loyalty
  • Enhances supply chain resilience and efficiency

Loan Eligibility

Check Your Eligibility

Before applying for a loan, ensure you qualify by reviewing the eligibility criteria and the list of required documents below.

Eligibility Criteria

Nationality Indian citizen
Minimum Age 21 years at the time of application
Maximum Age 70 years at loan maturity
Credit History Good credit history required
Constitution Type Self-employed individuals, Proprietorships, Partnerships, or Private Limited companies. Business must be operating for a minimum of 3 years.

Documents Required

Identity & Address Aadhaar Card and Business PAN
Proof of Business Business must be at least 1 year old with valid business proof
Bank Statement Current Account statement for 1 year
Financial Documents Audited financials and Income Tax Returns filed for the last 2 years
GST Returns GST return for at least 1 year
Existing Loans Sanction Letter and Statement of Account (SOA) for all loans from other financial institutions and banks
Property Documents Copy of title documents and approved sanction plan (for Loan Against Property)
