About Us

A Legacy of Trust in Financial Services

Artline Finance Pvt Ltd (formerly known as Artline Vinimay Pvt Ltd) is a trusted Non-Banking Finance Company (NBFC) established on 9 August 1996, registered with the Reserve Bank of India (Registration Certificate No. B-14.03540).

We are dedicated to empowering individuals and businesses with accessible, transparent, and innovative financial solutions. With a specific focus on SME projects, we provide a wide range of customized financial services — ensuring clients from all backgrounds receive the right financial tools to achieve stability, growth, and long-term success.

Our Mission

To make financial services straightforward, ethical, and accessible — bridging the gap between people and the financial resources they need for a secure future.

Our Vision

To become a leading financial solutions provider recognized for trust, innovation, and integrity — redefining how individuals and businesses approach financial planning.

Financing Solutions

Products We Offer

Our lending services empower businesses and individuals to unlock their financial potential with products designed for diverse needs.

🏠

Loan Against Property

Convert your property's value into accessible capital. Manage urgent financial needs with flexible tenures and no end-use restrictions on the loan amount.

  • Borrow up to 75% of the property's market value
  • Loan tenure of up to 15 years
  • No foreclosure charges
  • No end-use restrictions on the loan amount
  • Quick disbursal with doorstep services
💳

Unsecured Business Loans

Fuel your business growth with a loan tailored to your unique needs — from working capital to infrastructure improvement, new unit setup, and scaling production.

  • Quick initial assessment within a week of document submission
  • Evaluated on repayment capability and financial valuation
  • Applicable for startups as well
  • Working capital and expansion funding
  • Solutions for diverse business requirements
📊

Loan Against Shares

Unlock liquidity without selling your equity shares, mutual funds, or marketable securities. Access capital while your long-term investment portfolio remains intact.

  • No need to liquidate your equity shares
  • Continued dividend benefits during the loan period
  • Quick disbursal
  • Competitive rates and simple terms
  • Flexible for business or personal financial needs
🔗

Vendor & Supplier Finance

Empowering corporates to support their vendors and suppliers for a more efficient supply chain — financing against invoices, purchase orders, and work orders.

  • Loan against invoice, purchase order, or work order
  • Improved cash flow for suppliers
  • Reduced cost of capital compared to traditional lending
  • Strengthens supplier relationships and loyalty
  • Enhances supply chain resilience and efficiency

Loan Eligibility

Check Your Eligibility

Before applying for a loan, ensure you qualify by reviewing the eligibility criteria and the list of required documents below.

Eligibility Criteria

Nationality Indian citizen
Minimum Age 21 years at the time of application
Maximum Age 70 years at loan maturity
Credit History Good credit history required
Constitution Type Self-employed individuals, Proprietorships, Partnerships, or Private Limited companies. Business must be operating for a minimum of 3 years.

Documents Required

Identity & Address Aadhaar Card and Business PAN
Proof of Business Business must be at least 1 year old with valid business proof
Bank Statement Current Account statement for 1 year
Financial Documents Audited financials and Income Tax Returns filed for the last 2 years
GST Returns GST return for at least 1 year
Existing Loans Sanction Letter and Statement of Account (SOA) for all loans from other financial institutions and banks
Property Documents Copy of title documents and approved sanction plan (for Loan Against Property)

Corporate Social Responsibility
